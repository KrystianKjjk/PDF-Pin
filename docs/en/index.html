Every photo on the spot where it was taken.

Press and hold on a PDF plan — a pin appears. Take a photo and PDF Pin adds it as a new page of the same file, with location, time and a note. From the pin you jump to the photo, from the photo straight back to the plan.

01

How it works

  1. 1

    Press and hold

    A long press anywhere on the plan drops a numbered pin.

  2. 2

    Take a photo

    Camera or photo library. The app adds GPS, address, time and a note — which you can dictate.

  3. 3

    A new page

    The photo becomes a new page of the same PDF. No side folders, no guessing what was where.

  4. 4

    Both ways

    The pin leads to the photo, the photo page leads back to the plan. In the app and in any PDF reader.

No plan at hand? On the empty project list tap “See an example” — the app builds a demo project with a finished pin and an empty one to try.

03

Good to know

  • 01

    A plain PDF, no lock-in

    Pins are standard annotations, photos are pages, the way back is a link. Open the file in Preview, Acrobat or a browser and see what you saw in the app.

  • 02

    Works on a copy

    Importing creates a copy in the project folder. Your original stays untouched, and projects show up in the Files app as ordinary folders.

  • 03

    Your data stays on your phone

    No account, no server of ours, no analytics, no ads. We have no access to your files.

  • 04

    Google Drive — only if you want it

    Optional sync of project copies to your own Drive. The app sees only the files it created itself.

  • 05

    You decide what goes into the PDF

    Location, capture time and note on the photo page — each on its own, globally or for a single project.
