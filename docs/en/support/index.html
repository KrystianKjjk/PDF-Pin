How to report a bug from the app

  1. Open Settings (the gear on the project list).
  2. Under Diagnostics, choose Report a bug.
  3. Describe what happened and send — the message opens in your mail app.

The message contains your description, the app version, the iOS version, the Google Drive connection state and the number of projects, documents and pins.

You can attach a diagnostic log. It contains project and file names, but not their contents: no photos, notes or locations. Before anything is sent you see the whole message and tap “Send” yourself.

FAQ

Q01

Where are my files?

In the Files app → On My iPhone → PDF Pin. Every project is a folder: copies of the PDF documents and a photos subfolder with the pictures. The app works on a copy — the file you imported stays exactly as it was.

Q02

How do I delete a pin?

Touch and hold the pin on the drawing — its details open with a Delete pin button. Once you confirm, the pin, its photo page in the PDF and the photo file are removed. A single tap on a pin still takes you to its photo page.

Q03

Why is there no pin number 2?

A pin’s number never changes. When you delete a pin, a gap remains (1, 3, 4), because that number is already written into the PDF — on the plan and in the header of the photo page. Renumbering would make the app disagree with a document you may have already sent. A new pin gets the next number after the highest one.

Q04

Why does the app use GPS, and what if I say no?

Coordinates and an address are stored with the photo so you know where it was taken. The address is looked up by an Apple service. If you decline, pins and photos work as usual — just without a location. Change it in iOS Settings → PDF Pin → Location.

Q05

How does Google Drive sync work, and how do I turn it off?

It stays off until you sign in to your Google account on the Google Drive bar on the project list. Copies of PDFs and photos then go to PDF Pin/<project name> on your Drive — directly, not through any server of ours. The app sees only the files it created. To disconnect, tap the Google Drive bar → Disconnect. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Q06

How do I export the PDF with the photos?

Open the project and tap Share in the header — you get the system sheet with Mail, messengers and “Save to Files”. You export one finished PDF: the plan with pins and the photo pages with links, readable in any PDF reader. The same file is also in the Files app.

Q07

Do I need an account?

No. PDF Pin has no accounts and no sign-up. A Google account is needed only for the optional Drive sync.
